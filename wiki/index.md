# Index

Catalog of every wiki page. Regenerated by `Brain.rebuild_index()`.

Numbers live in `ledger/`, which is append-only. Pages here interpret the
ledger; they never restate it as fact.

_No synthesis pages yet. Drop a source into `raw/` and ask Cyrus to ingest it._

## How to read this vault

- `raw/` holds immutable sources. Filenames are `YYYY-MM-DD--slug.md` and each
  file carries a `source:` line. Nothing in here is ever edited.
- `wiki/` is Cyrus's own writing: entity pages, regime notes, playbooks, and
  post-mortems. Every claim is labelled `sourced`, `mixed`, or `assumption`.
- `ledger/` is the trade record, written by the kernel. Read-only to every
  agent, including Cyrus.

Open this folder as an Obsidian vault if you want the graph view. Obsidian is a
viewer here, not a dependency.
