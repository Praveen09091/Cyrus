# Log

Timeline of what the desk learned, newest last. Cyrus writes here; you read it.

- **2026-09-09** Desk scaffolded. Ten seats defined, message contract fixed, risk kernel made deterministic. Mode is paper and there is no live venue wired.
- **2026-09-09** Mean reversion gained a `min_edge_atr` filter. A z-score is scale-free, so a dead-quiet series was throwing large readings on pure noise and raising proposals worth less than the spread. The filter requires the trip back to the mean to beat one bar of normal movement.
- **2026-09-09** Synthetic `^VIX` given its own profile. Without it the generic base of 100 read as a permanent volatility crisis and pinned Atlas to a stress regime on every offline run.
- **2026-09-10** Backtest harness built (`cyrus/backtest/`). Decisions see only closed bars and fill at the next bar's open, gaps through a stop fill at the open rather than the level, and a bar that touches both stop and target resolves as a stop. Sizing runs through the live `size_proposal`, so a backtest cannot take a position the risk kernel would refuse.
- **2026-09-10** Walk-forward added, and with it the only honest source of an out-of-sample label. Parameters are selected on training folds and scored on the following unseen fold. With no grid to fit, a report labels itself in-sample and is barred from sizing, because a human still chose those parameters after looking at this market.
- **2026-09-10** **Leverage hole found and closed.** `max_book_exposure_pct` had been in the config since day one and was enforced nowhere. The first real SPY backtest paid $10,700 of fees on 30 trades, which only makes sense at roughly 4x equity in notional. Cause: a 1% risk cap divided by a tight ATR stop on a low-volatility instrument produces enormous size, and risk caps say nothing about leverage. `size_proposal` now converts the exposure limit into risk units so it competes in the same `min()`. Lesson: every configured limit needs a test that proves it binds, or it is a comment.
- **2026-09-10** Venue costs moved per book. A single 10bps commission was charging a commission-free US equity book a crypto taker fee, and it swamped the result: fees alone accounted for the entire SPY loss. Equities now carry regulatory fees plus a penny spread, crypto carries taker, India carries STT and the rest. The desk-wide fallback stays at the most expensive venue so a missing override is pessimistic. Config load now refuses an enabled book that trades at zero cost.
- **2026-09-10** Evidence is pooled per strategy, not per instrument. The first run filed SPY and QQQ under the same strategy name and the newest report silently erased the other. Records now pool every instrument a strategy trades, which is also the conservative reading: a clean SPY result cannot be shown without its losing QQQ sibling.
- **2026-09-10** First real out-of-sample results, all negative. Mean reversion on SPY and QQQ pooled to 77 trades, 49.4% win rate, net -$23.61 over three months of 15-minute bars. Momentum breakout lost on BTC and ETH. Kelly resolves to zero and the desk will not size any of it. This is the harness working: three months is a short sample and one regime, and the honest conclusion is that no strategy here has yet earned size.
